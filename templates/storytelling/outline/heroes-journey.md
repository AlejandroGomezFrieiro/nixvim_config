# The Hero's Journey

Twelve stations, Campbell-style. Not every story needs all of them — adapt
freely.

## Departure

- [ ] **Ordinary world** — a baseline that makes the change visible
- [ ] **Call to adventure** — a disruption, invitation, or refusal opportunity
- [ ] **Refusal of the call** — fear or duty; the question "why am I the one?"
- [ ] **Meeting the mentor** — wisdom, object, or one-line lesson
- [ ] **Crossing the threshold** — the point of no return; leave the known rules

## Initiation

- [ ] **Tests, allies, and enemies** — gathering the party and burning the plan
- [ ] **Approach to the inmost cave** — a pause that shows the true cost
- [ ] **Ordeal** — death-and-rebirth moment; the deepest fear faced
- [ ] **Reward (seizing the sword)** — claim what they came for, changed

## Return

- [ ] **The road back** — the pursuit; old vs new self frictions
- [ ] **Resurrection / final test** — the mastery of the ordeal, applied
- [ ] **Return with the elixir** — the gift brought home to the ordinary world

## Notes

- The **mentor** can be an object, a place, or an absence.
- The **refusal** can be skipped for characters who act without thinking.
- Keep the **elixir** concrete — an object *and* an idea.
