# The Story Circle (Dan Harmon)

Eight steps. Circular: each beat prepares the next; the end feeds the start.

1. [ ] **You** — the character in their familiar world (comfort)
2. [ ] **Need** — something calls them; the thing they *lack*
3. [ ] **Go** — they cross a threshold and cannot un-cross it
4. [ ] **Search** — trial and error; allies and enemies in the new world
5. [ ] **Find** — they take what they were looking for (it may not be the goal)
6. [ ] **Take** — the price: the thing claimed costs something
7. [ ] **Return** — they re-enter the familiar world, shaken
8. [ ] **Change** — the circle closed with a new normal

## Using the circle

- Run the circle once for the whole story, then again per act, then per scene:
  each level contains the larger one.
- **Find** and **Take** can be a single beat in tight scenes.
- The character at step 8 is *changed* — that's the emotional contract.
